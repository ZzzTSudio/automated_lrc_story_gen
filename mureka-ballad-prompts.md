# Mureka 9.5 抒情流行提示词整理

本文整理三套适合 Mureka 9.5 的英文音乐生成提示词。

---

## 版本一：Contemporary Mandopop

### 风格定位

- 当代华语流行
- 情绪直接、自然、旋律优先
- 主歌亲密口语化，副歌自然打开
- 配器克制，强调商业发行感

### Prompt

```text
Contemporary Mandopop, emotionally direct, polished and melody-first. Put a memorable Mandarin vocal melody and clear pop structure at the center. Use restrained live drums, warm bass, soft acoustic or clean electric guitar, subtle synth pads and limited piano support. Keep chord progressions familiar, smooth and mainstream, with gentle emotional lift into the chorus; avoid flashy reharmonization, obvious jazz/blues colors or overly ornate piano playing. Verses should feel intimate and conversational, while the chorus opens naturally with a strong singable hook and controlled emotional release. Keep all instrumental motion smooth and connected, with no jumpy fills, busy solos or sudden stylistic shifts. Around three minutes, natural lead vocal, balanced dynamics and release-ready commercial mix.
```

---

## 版本二：Contemporary Korean-language Pop Ballad

### 风格定位

- 现代韩语流行抒情或 K-pop 大抒情
- 极简、克制、高级、电影感
- 适度高级和声，但保持流行清晰度
- 旋律有少量意外转折，但仍然自然可唱
- 副歌宽阔、有持续音和明显情绪抬升

### Prompt

```text
Contemporary Korean-language pop ballad with a cinematic, polished and melody-first approach. Use a clear verse-pre-chorus-chorus structure and a memorable vocal melody with long lyrical phrases. Keep the verses intimate but rhythmically straight, using piano, soft acoustic guitar, warm bass and subtle pads. Build gradually into a wide, soaring chorus with strong melodic lift, sustained notes, tasteful strings, restrained live drums and backing harmonies. Use clear major and minor pop progressions with add2, sus4, occasional maj7 and subtle chromatic passing chords. Add a few unexpected melodic turns while keeping the melody natural and singable. Keep the groove steady and simple, with smooth connected transitions and controlled dynamics. Avoid swing, funk, syncopated phrasing, excessive vocal ornamentation, busy fills, trap elements, EDM drops and overdramatic orchestration. Around 3 minutes, natural lead vocal, spacious production and a polished modern mix.
```

## 版本三：Bossa Nova + Soft Rock Pop

### 风格定位

- 欢快、轻盈、明亮，有自然的夏日感
- 波萨诺瓦的切分尼龙吉他与轻打击乐
- 轻摇滚的现场乐队质感和开阔副歌
- 旋律优先，主歌松弛，副歌更有活力
- 避免变成重摇滚、爵士酒吧或过度拉丁化编曲

### Prompt

```text
Upbeat contemporary pop with a breezy bossa nova groove and soft rock energy. Keep the song bright, playful and melody-first, with a memorable Mandarin vocal melody and a clear verse-pre-chorus-chorus structure. Use syncopated nylon-string guitar, warm bass, light hand percussion, subtle brushed drums, clean electric guitar and gentle keys. Keep the verses relaxed and conversational, then let the chorus lift naturally with a catchy singable hook, open harmonies and tasteful backing vocals. Blend the Latin rhythm with a smooth live-band feel and a light rock chorus, keeping everything polished, warm and connected. Use familiar pop chords with occasional colorful add2, sus4 or maj7 touches. Avoid heavy rock distortion, aggressive drums, fast flashy solos, excessive jazziness, trap beats, abrupt transitions and overly dense production. Around 3 minutes, natural lead vocal, lively but balanced dynamics and a release-ready commercial mix.
```

## 三个版本的风格对比

| 版本 | 核心气质 | 旋律与副歌 | 主要配器 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Contemporary Mandopop | 温暖、直接、自然 | 好记、顺滑、控制释放 | 木吉他、电吉他、轻鼓、合成器 |
| Korean-language Pop Ballad | 克制、高级、电影感 | 长线条、宽阔上扬、明显高潮 | 钢琴、弦乐、铺底、层叠和声 |
| Bossa Nova + Soft Rock Pop | 欢快、轻盈、明亮 | 松弛主歌、活力副歌 | 尼龙吉他、手鼓、刷鼓、干净电吉他 |
| Contemporary Japanese J-pop | 明亮、丰富、青春感 | 鲜明灵活、高潮感强 | 现场鼓、钢琴、明亮电吉他、合成器 |
| Musical Theatre Ballad | 优雅、叙事、戏剧感 | 独白式主歌、舞台式释放 | 钢琴、竖琴、室内弦乐、钟琴 |
| Chinese Guofeng Pop Ballad | 诗意、婉转、东方感 | 细腻叙事、古风副歌 | 古筝、箫笛、琵琶、二胡、弦乐 |

## 使用建议

如果 Bossa Nova 版本过于爵士，可以删掉 `maj7`，并保留 `familiar pop chords`。如果摇滚感不够，可加入 `open electric guitar chorus`；如果节奏过于松散，可加入 `steady upbeat groove`。

## 版本六：Contemporary Chinese Guofeng Pop Ballad

### 风格定位

- 主流古风抒情，强调诗意、叙事和东方审美
- 参考古风流行的共同音乐特征，不对应某一位具体歌手
- 以古筝、箫/笛子、琵琶、二胡、钢琴和室内弦乐为主要音色
- 五声音阶结合现代流行和声
- 主歌细腻叙事，副歌婉转打开，结尾有电影感抬升
- 避免戏曲腔、武侠快歌、电子舞曲和过度炫技

### Prompt

```text
Contemporary Chinese guofeng pop ballad, poetic, elegant and emotionally restrained. Center the song on expressive Mandarin vocals, lyrical storytelling and a refined melody. Use a clear verse-pre-chorus-chorus structure, instrumental intro and emotional final chorus. Build around guzheng, xiao or dizi, pipa, erhu, warm piano, chamber strings and restrained traditional percussion. Blend a Chinese pentatonic foundation with smooth modern pop harmony, tasteful suspended or add9 chords and subtle modal color. Keep verses delicate and narrative, then let the chorus open with flowing vocal lines, graceful ornamentation and layered harmonies. Add instrumental answers between vocal phrases and a gentle cinematic lift. Avoid opera-style singing, excessive vocal runs, martial-arts energy, electronic beats, EDM drops, rock distortion, flashy solos and dense orchestration. Around 3 to 4 minutes, expressive Mandarin lead vocal, poetic atmosphere, organic dynamics and a polished guofeng mix.
```

```text
Contemporary Chinese guofeng pop ballad, cinematic and tragic, expressive female vocal, pentatonic melody, guzheng and erhu over piano and orchestral strings, restrained war drums, spacious verses, soaring anthemic chorus, dramatic dynamic build, snow-cold atmosphere, polished modern pop production.
```

## 版本五：Contemporary Musical Theatre Ballad

### 风格定位

- 偏现代音乐剧抒情曲，强调叙事和戏剧推进
- 钢琴作为和声与节奏核心
- 竖琴、室内弦乐、钟琴和少量管弦打击乐作为主要色彩
- 主歌像角色独白，副歌像舞台上的情绪释放
- 不使用吉他、合成器主音或电子节拍
- 避免唱成歌剧，也避免过度炫技和过度煽情

### Prompt

```text
Contemporary musical theatre ballad, elegant, cinematic and story-driven. Center the song on expressive Mandarin vocal storytelling, clear diction, a theatrical melody and dramatic arc: intimate verse, rising pre-chorus, expansive chorus, bridge and final reprise. Use piano as the harmonic foundation, with delicate harp arpeggios, chamber strings, subtle celesta and light orchestral percussion. Grow organically from sparse accompaniment to an emotional climax, with lyrical counter-melodies and layered vocal harmonies. Use refined tonal harmony with clear cadences, occasional suspended chords and gentle chromatic movement, keeping the music singable and dramatic. Avoid guitars, synth leads, electronic beats, heavy drum grooves, jazz improvisation, excessive vocal runs and operatic over-singing. Keep the production warm, spacious, organic and stage-like. Around 3 to 4 minutes, natural Mandarin lead vocal, strong narrative phrasing, controlled dynamics and a polished theatrical mix.
```

## 版本四：Contemporary Japanese J-pop

### 风格定位

- 明亮、丰富、有青春感和情绪推动力
- 旋律鲜明，段落清晰，副歌具有高记忆点
- 现场乐队质感结合闪亮的钢琴与合成器
- 主歌灵活轻快，预副歌逐渐上升
- 避免变成 EDM、Trap、过度爵士或沉闷极简风格

### Prompt

```text
Bright contemporary Japanese J-pop, melodic, colorful and uplifting. Center the song on a distinctive Japanese vocal melody with clear phrasing and a strong pop structure: verse, pre-chorus, chorus, bridge and final chorus. Use energetic live drums, warm bass, clean electric guitar, acoustic guitar, sparkling piano, light synths and subtle strings. Keep the verses nimble and conversational, then build through a rising pre-chorus into a wide, anthemic chorus with a memorable hook, layered harmonies and dynamic melodic movement. Use familiar pop harmony with tasteful add2, sus4, maj7 and occasional chromatic passing chords. Add playful rhythmic changes, bright guitar figures and a short instrumental lift while keeping the arrangement coherent. Avoid heavy EDM drops, trap beats, excessive jazziness, gloomy minimalism, harsh distortion and complex solos. Around 3 minutes, natural Japanese lead vocal, lively dynamics, polished modern production and a release-ready J-pop mix.
```
